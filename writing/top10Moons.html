The back cover

Jesse Walker has a secret: he’s the top assassin and fixer for The Board, a global organization overseeing the collection and distribution of energy. He only kills when he has to, and he dreams about the day he can escape the chaos.

When Robin Sykes, an engineer at HNTR systems, creates life out of nothing—a little red fox—he’s sent to shut down the project. But as they keep secrets from each other while their friendship evolves into an unexpected romance, they have to navigate a centuries-long feud between two megacorporations while uncovering an even more sinister plot as deadly forces close in.

Inspired by the video games, books, and metal/rock music, Top 10 Moons that Cried Blood is a dynamic and addictive sci-fi romance set in San Francisco.

What's in a name?

The title is a nod to “Top 10 staTues tHat CriEd bloOd” by Bring Me the Horizon, my favorite band. I name a lot of stuff after my favorite songs.

About the author, per the back cover

Eddie Yang wrote his debut novel in between games of ranked Overwatch 2. He lives with his pet gecko Sparky, and has received the “At least mid, probably” award from the Library Association’s Guild Club of Reading Books.
